Made for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, “Interplanetary Survival Guide: Martian Map”. NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026「Interplanetary Survival Guide: Martian Map」のために作りました。

Dataデータ

  • MRO HiRISE — MSL Gale DEM & Orthophoto Mosaics, PSP_009650_1755 Terrain, safety, easy-to-locate地形、安全性、迷いにくさ
  • MRO CRISM — MTRDR FRT0000B6F1 Hematiteヘマタイト
  • Viking Orbiters — MDIM 2.1 colour mosaic The globe on the home screenホーム画面の火星
  • MGS MOLA — MEGDR global elevation The far landscape遠くの地形
  • Curiosity (MSL) — localized waypoints, REMS weather The rover's path, the science targets, weather探査車の道、科学目標、天気

Methods手法

  • Perseverance self-localization (NASA JPL)
  • GESTALT — Goldberg, Maimone & Matthies, 2002
  • Tobler, 1993 · Pandolf, Givoni & Goldman, 1977
  • Prentice & Roy, 2009 · Apollo Lunar Surface Journal
  • Mars24 (NASA GISS)

Softwareソフトウェア

  • Space Grotesk · JetBrains Mono
