MARSWALK

        
Gale Crater, Mars. There is no GPS here, and a compass does not work.

        

          EVA-1: The Lake Rocks. Walk about 700 m from the rover to Vera Rubin Ridge and collect a hematite
          sample. NASA's CRISM instrument saw hematite here from orbit; it often forms in water.
        

        

        
        

          Real terrain and imagery: NASA/JPL-Caltech/UArizona/USGS (MRO HiRISE). Minerals: MRO CRISM. Route: MSL Curiosity.
